## Summary

### Pages with generation-HIGH content
These pages change the pixels GPT Image 2 would produce and are the priority extraction targets:

- **Page 03 — COLOR_SPEC (the single most valuable page):** named, exact-coded palette — 山野绿 (mountain green), 金盖黄 (marigold gold), 稻米白 (rice-grain white) + deep-brown accent. The only page with hex/RGB/CMYK.
- **Page 12 — Super symbol** rendered with gold ingot/clay-pot texture on warm dark still-life (mood + symbol appearance).
- **Page 15 — Cultural DNA mood-board** (clay pot / incense / scholar / god-of-wealth / chicken): defines the *subject vocabulary*.
- **Page 16 — Auxiliary woodcut icon set** (flat olive folk icons): defines the *illustration style / motif library*.
- **Page 22 — Main key-visual** (linocut/woodblock 3×3 grid, dark-brown-on-cream): the canonical *illustration style reference*.
- **Page 23 — KV/packaging illustration variants** (scholar-with-skewers + rooster silhouettes).
- **Pages 24, 25, 28 — Photographic posters/billboards:** define the *brand photography style* (rustic farm, harvest, flaming clay-pot, ingredient-provenance, warm natural light).
- **Page 37 — Takeout illustration** (mounted-figure woodcut on kraft+sage).

Secondary (MEDIUM) but useful: logotype/clerical-script pages 05–10, symbol construction 13, interior/exterior render mood 29–33, soft-furnishing motif applications 35–40.

### Recommended NEW extractable fields (beyond color / fonts / motifs / slogan)
This document supports adding the following to the Brand DNA spec — each is something that demonstrably changes generated pixels:

1. **illustration_style** — e.g. "woodcut / linocut / folk block-print, flat single-color (dark brown or olive) on cream, distressed texture." Pages 16, 22, 23, 37 make this unambiguous and it is arguably the strongest single generation signal in the whole deck.
2. **logotype_script_style** — "custom 隶书 (clerical script), slanted-pen tension, traditional-meets-modern strokes." Pages 05–07. Useful if GPT Image 2 is asked to render brand-consistent Chinese type.
3. **photography_style / scene_mood** — "rustic farm-to-table, warm natural daylight, clay-pot-over-fire, bamboo/courtyard/brick settings, shallow depth of field." Pages 05, 15, 24, 25, 28.
4. **lighting_mood (renders)** — "warm amber edge-glow on beige façade against deep-navy twilight; wet reflective pavement." Pages 29–33. A reusable lighting descriptor.
5. **material_palette / texture** — "weathered/distressed surfaces, kraft paper, natural canvas, rattan, sage-upholstery, copper/champagne-gold metal, pulp takeout." Pages 32, 35, 37, 38, 40.
6. **subject_iconography** — discrete renderable subjects to feature: clay pot (砂锅), gold ingot (元宝), rooster/free-range chicken (土鸡), scholar (秀才), god of wealth (财神), incense (烧香), flaming wok. Pages 08, 13, 15, 16, 22.
7. **logo_container_shape** — "distressed clay-pot / bowl / cloud-rounded badge silhouette enclosing the wordmark; gold cloud-outline badges." Pages 09, 10, 19, 20, 36 — affects how a generated logo lockup looks.
8. **color_role_mapping** — which color is ground vs. ink vs. accent (olive-green grounds, cream/rice-white grounds, gold accent + brown ink for illustration). Inferable across the deck; pairs with the page-03 palette.
9. **accent_metal_finish** — "champagne/antique gold with silver bevel, embossed 3D" for marks/badges (page 35) — a finish cue for emblem generation.

### Fields the document does NOT meaningfully support
Clear-space mm, minimum-size px, improper-use prohibition grids, and Pantone callouts are **absent** — this is an atmospheric proposal, not a rule-bound VI manual. Spec pages other than page 03 carry no machine values, so do not expect a structured measurement layer.
